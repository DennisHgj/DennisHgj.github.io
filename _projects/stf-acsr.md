STF-ACSR uses a multimodal large language model to identify informative hand keyframes, followed by a lightweight fusion strategy that combines hand cues with visual speech representations.

The approach minimizes task-specific training while retaining the complementary hand and lip information that defines cued speech. It was accepted at ICASSP 2026.
