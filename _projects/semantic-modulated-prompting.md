Semantic Modulated Prompting (SMP) adapts audio-visual models to few-shot recognition using semantic adapters, prompt latent attention, and prompted prototype regularization.

The method focuses on efficient transfer under limited labeled data and was published in IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing in 2026.

- [Publication](https://doi.org/10.1109/TASLPRO.2026.3654246)
