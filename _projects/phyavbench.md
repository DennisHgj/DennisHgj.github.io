PhyAVBench evaluates whether generated videos respect physical interactions and maintain cross-modal consistency between sound and vision. Its controlled design uses 1,000 paired prompts with more than 20 generated videos per comparison group.

I contributed as a core author. The work was selected for an oral presentation at ACM Multimedia 2026.

- [Project page](https://imxtx.github.io/PhyAVBench/)
- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23994)
