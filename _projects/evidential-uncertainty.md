This project develops evidential query-level uncertainty estimators for in-context learning, including E-UQ-T and E-UQ-T-F. The approach has been evaluated across five datasets and multiple LLaMA and Qwen model families.

In the evaluated setting, uncertainty-guided inference reduced average token consumption by 96.7% while preserving task-oriented decision quality.
