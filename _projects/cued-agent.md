Cued-Agent coordinates specialized agents for visual perception, hand-cue integration, recognition, and language-level correction. It is designed to improve cued speech recognition without task-specific end-to-end training.

The project also includes a Mandarin cued speech dataset collected for speakers with hearing impairment. The work was selected for an oral presentation at ACM Multimedia 2025 and received a Student Travel Award.
